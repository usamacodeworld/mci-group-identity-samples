Design rationale

Why we designed Option A

MCI Group sits above Consultation, Academy, Career, and Research. Option A uses the same “people / care” language as career.mci.med.sa so the holding brand and platforms feel like one family — useful for social launch and cross-platform marketing.

What it represents

  • Circle (head) — the human at the centre of care
  • Twin wings — support, partnership, and global reach
  • Blue + green — trust (medical) + growth / vitality (care)
  • Open upward form — progress and a healthier future

Why this direction

Best when MCI Group should feel warm, human, and aligned with Career. Easy to recognise on shirts, social avatars, and cards because the mark is simple and already proven on a live MCI platform.

Option B

M monogram

Bold geometric “M” with a green care signal. Strong holding-company look — distinct from Career’s twin-wing, still in the same blue/green system.

Distinct Group mark
00 · Case study
Design rationale

Why we designed Option B

A holding company often needs a mark that is clearly “Group” — not the same icon as one platform. Option B builds a strong M monogram so MCI Group owns a unique lettermark while platforms keep their own metaphors (globe, academy, wings, research).

What it represents

  • Letter M — MCI; memorable monogram for a group brand
  • Pathway / peaks — clinical journeys and rising standards
  • Green care dot — life, signal, and human care inside the system
  • Blue gradient — depth, trust, and medical authority

Why this direction

Best when Group should look more corporate and distinct from Career. The monogram works well on embroidery, favicons, and decks where a single letter reads faster than a complex symbol.

Option C

Medical shield

Shield + cross + globe arc — classic healthcare trust. Reads instantly as medical; good if Group should feel more institutional than Career.

Healthcare authority
00 · Case study
Design rationale

Why we designed Option C

International medical groups are often recognised through symbols of protection and healing. Option C uses a shield, cross, and globe arc so MCI Group reads instantly as a trusted healthcare organisation — especially for partners, hospitals, and Arabic/English audiences who expect clear medical cues.

What it represents

  • Shield — protection, reliability, and patient safety
  • Medical cross — care, treatment, and clinical purpose
  • Globe arc — international network and global pathways
  • Green accent — hope, recovery, and healthier communities

Why this direction

Best when Group should feel institutional and medically authoritative — closer to hospital / network branding than to a lifestyle or career brand. Strong for official documents, partnerships, and print stationery.
